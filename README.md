AI Solutions Development Coding Exercises

The Applied A.I. Solutions Development program is one of the first of its kind offered by a Canadian college. Developed by George Brown College's School of Computer Technology, the program acknowledges the industry demand for full-stack data scientists. Graduates of this program are prepared to take on roles such as Machine Learning Engineer

Additionally, students develop business analysis skills, enabling them to effectively communicate the application of artificial intelligence (A.I.), machine learning (ML), and deep learning (DL) in various business models.
